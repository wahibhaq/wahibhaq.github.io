# Review of Design Jam @ Vectorform, Munich


I attended a day long event on 22nd April known as **[Design Jam](http://designj.am/)** here in Munich. It was quite an amazing experience for me as a developer. Funny enough I was always shortlisted for past 3 years but never managed to actually attend it but finally (quite gladly!) managed this time. Just wanted to write a short post about the event and some of my learnings before I forget :) 

[Design Jam](http://designj.am/) is basically a one-day event that brings together Designers, Developers and Entrepreneurs, to solve a problem and build a prototype in just 10 hours (or so). Each Jam’s theme is announced only on the morning of the event in order to maintain maximum excitement. It was started by [Rachel](https://twitter.com/rilan) who is currently working as UX Designer in the Chrome UX team. You can check out her work on her personal [website](http://www.rachelilansimpson.com/).  

This time the theme for participants was "How to innovate furniture building process using Microsoft HoloLens". In Europe/US, it's quite common to receive blocks of furniture which you have to assemble yourself and if you have done it yourself you'll agree that it's an extremely tedious and boring task. Every attendee had a good understanding of the problem because of personal experience with world's famous IKEA furnitures which come with a paper manual. Dismantling a furniture item is a much bigger problem and requires more care and attention. Coincidently, I was already planning to dismantle a wardrobe from friend's place and assemble it back in my apartment after 2 days from the event date. Later, the Design Jam was somewhat helpful with mental preparation what to expect 😔 

Anyways coming back to event, it started with short intro about event and mandatory sponsorship message from [Vectorform](https://www.vectorform.com/). Vectorform is a pretty cool and innovative company here in Munich and by the way, they are looking for [kick-ass designers](https://vectorform.applicantpro.com/jobs/454506.html) and [business development manager](https://vectorform.applicantpro.com/jobs/464275.html). So after the intro session, they revealed the theme which was basically about **Finding innovative solutions to furniture assembly using Augmented Reality (AR) Technology**. I am quite aware of how Vectorform closely works with AR and Virtual Reality (VR) interfaces so it wasn't a surprise for me. The recommended AR platform was Microsoft HoloLens and we were also given a basic demo of it. The solutions were supposed to be not limited to capabilities of HoloLens but it was meant to give a good idea of limitations and real-life usecase.

![intro-session](https://github.com/wahibhaq/wahibhaq.github.io/blob/designjam-post/img/blog/posts/design-jam-vectorform-2017/intro-session.jpg)

  

### Team Formation

We were supposed to create a team of max 5 person and should be of diverse background. Every participant had a badge stating whether he/she was a designer, engineer or business. I ended up forming a team of 3 including Sebastian (business guy | Allianz) and Charlota (designer | IDEO). Those who are aware of the term, "Design Thinking" definitely know about IDEO being at the forefront of creating change through design. So I was already excited to work and learn from her.  

![Our Team: Me (left), Sebastian (middle) and Charlota (right)](https://github.com/wahibhaq/wahibhaq.github.io/blob/designjam-post/img/blog/posts/design-jam-vectorform-2017/our-team.jpg)


### What exactly happened during the hands-on work

Here's the schedule which was published on eventbrite page:

#### 1st Brainstorming session (20 - 30 min)

In 1st Brainstorming session, each team member was supposed to do some quick sketches of 4-8 different ideas (whatever comes in mind) or just write them down. Then all of different ideas were evaluaed and distinct themes were listed down so that we can get an overall idea of various themes. 

Later those ideas were prioritized within the team using green colored markers. Excuse my bad writing but these were the ideas which we compiled. All of us agreed that core problems faced by users revolve around assembling furniture is tedius, boring, instruction manuals not being sufficient in guidance and organizing components being a big hindrance.

Top 3 ideas were: 

* Gamification / Fun : Find ways to make the experience more fun 
* Live Queues / Tutorial / Chatbot : Give live instructions via tutorial or chatbot to help user in clearly understanding the next steps to do
* Organize Components / Tools : Find ways to help user in organizing all tools and components which come in a box to make it convenient to use them.

#### 2nd Brainstorming session (20 - 30 min)

In this phase, every team member was supposed to choose one of the three or a hybrid theme out of all these to create a sketch and showcase a proposed solution. It was supposed to be quite focused on the choosen theme and to extensively think and imagine how we think the solution should look like. We were open to either create sketches or write in form of points. Later the ideas were dot-voted and direction was decided.

Frankly, It's embarassing to confess but it was my first experience to create such a sketch but it was an absolute fun. I mean I am moderately good wth pencil sketches of faces (an old hobbie) but this was more tough than I expected. This was also one of the reasons why I wanted to attend this Design Jam to force myself to do stuff never done before. I realized how differently engineers and designers perceive a solution and we definitely suck (quite a stereotype) at sketching :)

Out of all, Charlota did an amazing job for all the good reasons.

#### Team Discussion

It was followed by Team discussion & finalizing of idea to be followed for rest of the day.

#### Start Building Prototype

After lunch, we were asked to focus completely on building our prototype. The good thing was that there were no limitations on how and what to show. It could be a sketch, paper prototype, htmlclick dummy, a website, a video about your service or anything else. So, it gave a lot of space for experimentation and creativity.

#### Testing with an advisor and getting feedback

Then we were asked to test it among ourselves in presence of an advisor to find pain points, mistakes and improvements.

- We then focused on addressing the pain points and improvements in our Prototype.

#### Final Presentation 

Later at the end, every team presented their prorotype in front of the audience within limit of 10 minutes.
